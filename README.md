# Baba-V8
This repository contains the custom flight controller computer namely Baba V8 designed by me with Saraç Rocket Team.
I serve as Deputy Head of Avionics Department of Saraç Rocket Team, while my main role is to develop embedded systems.
Baba V8 is developed to take place in TEKNOFEST 2026 Low Altitude Rocket Competition.
The board consists of ATmega2560 Microprocessor, BNO055 Gyroscope, MPL3115A2 Barometer, L86-M33 GPSS/GNSS and Ebyte E220-400T30S Telemtry modules.
To upload the bootloader/firmware, it's recommended to use the Type-C port.
Have a safe flight.
